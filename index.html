Core flow

1) Choose Track Type (player choice). This is your intent for the Room.

2) Roll Curse Check (random). If it says Target or Mix, roll that curse next.

3) Roll Mutation (random). Apply the constraint while building that Room’s track.

4) Roll Power-Up (optional/random). Track your Power-Ups and spend at most one per Room.

Track Type (chosen)

Track Type is the only thing you choose. It sets the role of what you’re making this Room (drums, bass, chords, etc.).

Everything else (curses, mutations, power-ups) is determined by rolls.

Curse Check (random)
Mutations (random)
A mutation is a constraint on how you make the current Room’s track.
Target Curses (random)
Target curses affect one specific track.
Mix Curses (random)
Mix curses affect the whole project/workflow.
Power-Ups (random)

Power-Ups are a limited resource. Track them with the counter.

  • You can gain Power-Ups by rolling them or using the +1/-1 buttons.
  • Suggested rule: use at most one Power-Up per Room (this app tracks that).
  • When you use one, apply the effect per your table/rules (this app logs the use).
